Companies are like organic entities, made up of people. For a company to turn into a great one, people working in the company have to turn great too. 

Claire, having background in politics, tech, and entrepreneurship, puts together a list of advice that systemizes the process of scaling people, and thus the company.

It is a solid seven. I didn't put a higher score because the book is not entirely inclusive. It is written for people who are already (actively) managing teams or a number of teams. But regardless, I found most of the advice real-time practical, despite [Agora](https://www.agorawriting.com) not being a big-team project.
