Hi, I'm Husan.

Learning, building, and living my life in my hometown, Tashkent.

I like the idea of [books](/reading) widely. I sometimes publish [essays](/writing) on economic innovation, artificial intelligence, and systems of any sort. Exposure -> Introspection is the best way to learn, I believe.

Reading and writing won't be enough at times. I study the subject inside out if I need to. I do deep dives.

When studying becomes too boring, I may try tweaking a thing or two in software and hardware - of course, to ship something real. In doing so, I build [projects](/projects) to share a little of what I know with others.

I believe in the Greek concept of kalokagathia: the unity of fitness with intellect. So I try to keep my body in an athletic state. I often run. I have been to multiple local and international marathon races in the past. Check out my running club: [Agora Runs](https://runs.agorawriting.com).

I also play tennis. I want to take it seriously and try my luck in a few contests, but committing to it fully has been hard in this era of daily skirmishes with capitalism.

You may also find me posting reflections and Polaroid pictures from my treks. Got into trekking a year ago. I've been obsessed ever since. And just like any self-respected trekker would, I am also collecting my gear one by one: Bought the Salomons, the Arc'teryx shell jacket is underway.

I don't usually like labels - be it on me or others. But the above are one or two things that I usually agree with when people say things about me.
